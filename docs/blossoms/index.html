Cherry blossoms in Victoria, BC

Victoria has 5,375 flowering cherry and plum trees on public land, and 805 more magnolias, crabapples, pears and serviceberries. Blossom season starts with the winter-flowering cherries, peaks with the cherry plums and Yoshino cherries in March, and ends with the Japanese cherries and crabapples in early May.

On the map they're coloured by their flowers: white, pale pink, pink, deep pink.
