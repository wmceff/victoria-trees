Hybrid yew Taxus x

A hybrid of English and Japanese yews, first raised in Massachusetts in the early 1900s.

The only one on the map.

Show Hybrid yew tree on the map

Hybrid yew, also called Anglojap yew, was first raised by the horticulturist T. D. Hatfield. It grows slowly and is not harmed by frequent clipping, which makes it a popular hedge and a good subject for bonsai. Like all yews, its needles, branches and seeds contain taxines, which are toxic to the heart.
