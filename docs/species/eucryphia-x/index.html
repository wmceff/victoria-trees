Rostrevor eucryphia Eucryphia x

A hybrid of a Chilean and a Tasmanian eucryphia, first raised at Rostrevor in Northern Ireland.

A rare one here: only 2 on the map (#263 of 368 species).

Show Rostrevor eucryphia trees on the map

Rostrevor eucryphia blooms in late summer and autumn, with sweetly scented flowers 3–6 cm (1–2 in) across: four creamy-white petals around a mass of stamens. Tasmanian leatherwood, one of its wild relatives, is a major source of honey. The name Eucryphia comes from the Greek for "well-covered", for the leaves clustered at the branch tips.
