Hawthorn maple Acer crataegifolium

Native to the mountain forests of central and southern Japan.

The only one on the map.

Show Hawthorn maple tree on the map

Hawthorn maple's Japanese name, urikaede, means "melon maple", for its green bark with pale stripes like a melon's skin. Its botanical name means "hawthorn-leaved", a likeness that holds only for certain hawthorns. Charles Maries of the Veitch nursery brought it to England in 1879, but it is hard to propagate and seldom grown.
