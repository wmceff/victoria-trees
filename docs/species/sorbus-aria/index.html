Whitebeam takes its name from the almost-white undersides of its leaves; "beam" is an old English word for a tree. Its scarlet berries are edible only when overripe, or bletted. Botanists now place the whitebeams in a genus of their own, Aria, apart from the rowans of Sorbus, so this tree is also listed as Aria edulis.
