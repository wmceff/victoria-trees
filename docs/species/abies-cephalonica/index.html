Greek Fir Abies cephalonica

Native to the mountains of southern Greece, including the island of Kefalonia.

A rare one here: only 4 on the map (#223 of 368 species).

Show Greek Fir trees on the map

Greek fir is named after Cephalonia (Kefalonia), the largest of the Ionian Islands. Once an important timber tree in Greece, it is now too rare to be of much value, and bark beetles have killed many trees in recent hot, dry years. It is one of the first conifers to put out new shoots in spring, so late frosts can damage it.
