Southern magnolia Magnolia grandiflora

Native to the southeastern United States.

29 on the map, the #117 most common of 384 species.

Show Southern magnolia trees on the map

Squirrels, opossums, quail, and turkey are known to eat the seeds.

Over 150 cultivars have been developed and named, although only 30 to 40 of these still exist and still fewer are commercially propagated and sold.
