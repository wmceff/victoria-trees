Weeping Golden Willow Salix × sepulcralis 'Chrysocoma'

A rare one here: only 9 on the map (#188 of 384 species).

Show Weeping Golden Willow trees on the map

It is an artificial hybrid between S. alba 'Vitellina' and S. babylonica. The first parent provides the frost hardiness and the golden shoots and the second parent the strong weeping habit.
