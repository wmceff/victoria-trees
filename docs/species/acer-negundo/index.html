Manitoba Maple Acer negundo

Native to much of North America, from the Canadian Prairies south to Central America.

Wildlife: Evening grosbeaks eat its seeds.

A rare one here: only 4 on the map (#223 of 368 species).

Show Manitoba Maple trees on the map

Manitoba maple's other name, box elder, comes from its pale wood, like boxwood, and its leaves, divided into three to seven leaflets like an elder's; no other maple regularly has more than three. The oldest surviving wooden flutes in the Americas, found in northeastern Arizona and dated to about 620–670 CE, are made of box elder.
