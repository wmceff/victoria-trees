Chinese pistache Pistacia chinensis

Native to most of China, and Taiwan.

11 on the map, the #175 most common of 368 species.

Show Chinese pistache trees on the map

Chinese pistache is the most frost-hardy of the pistachio trees, surviving about −25 °C (−13 °F), and is used as a rootstock for the edible pistachio, Pistacia vera. Male and female flowers grow on separate trees, and the female trees produce small red fruits that turn blue when ripe.
