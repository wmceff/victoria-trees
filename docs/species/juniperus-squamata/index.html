Flaky juniper Juniperus squamata

Native to the Himalayas and China, from Afghanistan to Taiwan.

The only one on the map.

Show Flaky juniper tree on the map

Flaky juniper is named for its flaking brown bark. It grows high in the mountains of Asia, up to 4,900 m (16,100 ft), and is the unofficial provincial tree of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, in Pakistan. In gardens it is grown for its blue-green foliage and compact shape, in forms such as 'Blue Star'.
