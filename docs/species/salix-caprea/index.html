Kilmarnock Willow Salix caprea

Native to Europe and western and central Asia.

Wildlife: Moth and butterfly caterpillars feed on its leaves.

The only one on the map.

Show Kilmarnock Willow tree on the map

Goat willow probably gets its name from the first known picture of it, in Hieronymus Bock's herbal of 1546, which shows a goat browsing on it. Its silky catkins are the pussy willows of early spring, and in countries from Germany to Ukraine and Finland the just-opened catkins are carried in place of olive branches on Palm Sunday.
