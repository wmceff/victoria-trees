Eastern hemlock Tsuga canadensis

Native to eastern North America, from the Great Lakes and the Maritimes south along the Appalachians.

A rare one here: only 4 on the map (#223 of 368 species).

Show Eastern hemlock trees on the map

Eastern hemlock is the state tree of Pennsylvania, and its bark was once a major source of tannin for tanning leather. Across much of its range it is threatened by the hemlock woolly adelgid, a sap-sucking insect from East Asia, and a Pacific Northwest beetle that eats the adelgid has been released in New York to control it.
