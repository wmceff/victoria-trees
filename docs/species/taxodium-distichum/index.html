Bald cypress Taxodium distichum

Native to the southeastern United States.

The City of Victoria has 5 trees of this species on record, 0.01% of the 35,125 trees on the map.

Show Bald cypress trees on the map

Bald cypress trees are deciduous (not evergreen) and when growing in swamps have a peculiarity of growth called cypress knees - woody projections from the root system project above the ground or water. It is still unknown what purpose these serve.
