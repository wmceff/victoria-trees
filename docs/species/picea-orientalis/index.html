Oriental spruce Picea orientalis

Native to the Caucasus and northeastern Turkey, near the Black Sea.

A rare one here: only 4 on the map (#223 of 368 species).

Show Oriental spruce trees on the map

Oriental spruce has the shortest needles of any spruce, just 6–8 mm (about 1/4 in) long and glossy dark green, while its Himalayan relative, the morinda spruce, has the longest. Its young cones are green or red to purple, ripening dark brown within months. It is a popular tree for large gardens in northern Europe and the United States.
