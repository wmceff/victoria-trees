Black Hawthorn Crataegus douglasii

Native to northern and western North America, including Victoria.

131 on the map, the #57 most common of 384 species.

Show Black Hawthorn trees on the map

Hard wood used by First Peoples for digging sticks, hammers, and implement handles.

It is named after David Douglas, who collected seed from the plant during his botanical explorations.
