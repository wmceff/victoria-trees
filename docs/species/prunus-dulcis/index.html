Almond Prunus dulcis

Native to Iran and surrounding countries.

A rare one here: only 2 on the map (#263 of 368 species).

Show Almond trees on the map

Almond was one of the first fruit trees to be domesticated, because good trees can be raised from seed without grafting, and almonds were found in Tutankhamun's tomb, from about 1325 BCE. In the Bible, Aaron's rod brings forth almond flowers, and the cups of the menorah in the Temple were shaped like almond blossoms.
