Manchurian striped maple Acer tegmentosum

Native to the Russian Far East, northeastern China and Korea.

The only one on the map.

Show Manchurian striped maple tree on the map

Manchurian striped maple has greenish-grey bark with bright white stripes, and its leaves turn bright yellow in autumn. It survives winters as cold as about −29 °C (−20 °F). It is not generally considered at risk, but China's 2004 red list of species classed it as vulnerable.
