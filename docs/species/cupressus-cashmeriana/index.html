Kashmir cypress / Bhutan cypress Cupressus cashmeriana

Native to the eastern Himalaya in Bhutan and adjacent areas of Arunachal Pradesh in northeastern India.

A rare one here: only 5 on the map (#217 of 384 species).

Show Kashmir cypress / Bhutan cypress trees on the map

It is the official national tree of Bhutan, where it is often associated with Buddhist religious places. It has been widely planted around Vihara monasteries and Buddhist temples there for centuries.
