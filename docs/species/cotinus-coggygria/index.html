Smoke tree Cotinus coggygria

Native to southern Europe, central Asia and the Himalayas, east to northern China.

A rare one here: only 4 on the map (#223 of 368 species).

Show Smoke tree trees on the map

Smoke tree is named for its feathery plumes: most of its flowers fail and lengthen into pinkish wisps that look like smoke when seen together. Its wood once gave a yellow dye, young fustic, and like other sumacs it has been used for red dyes, as in Middle Eastern soumak rugs.
