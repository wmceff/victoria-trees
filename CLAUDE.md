# Victoria Trees

- `pipeline/` builds `docs/data/trees.json` and the static pages from the City of Victoria's open
  data plus the curated files in `data/`. Run it with `npm run build` in `pipeline/`.
- `docs/` is the published site (GitHub Pages). `docs/index.html` and `docs/script.js` are
  hand-written; the species, neighbourhood, blossom and about pages are generated by
  `pipeline/pages.mjs`.
- Curated species details, cultivar notes and Turner page references live in `data/species.yml`.

When writing or editing species or cultivar descriptions, follow this guide:

@data/description-style.md
